Colorbond® Steel Fencing

Australia’s most popular boundary fencing system for Illawarra homes.

COLORBOND® steel fencing is the go-to choice for homeowners across Wollongong, Shellharbour and Kiama who want privacy, durability and a clean finish that looks good from both sides. Panels are manufactured from pre-painted BlueScope steel designed for Australian conditions — including coastal salt air common along the Illawarra. When installed correctly there are no vertical gaps, no loose palings and minimal footholds, which improves both privacy and security. We supply and install a full range of panel styles and can match popular colour ranges so your fence coordinates with roofing, gutters or existing steelwork. Matching pedestrian and driveway gates complete the system for a seamless boundary. Best for: residential side and rear boundaries, estate homes, privacy between neighbours, low-maintenance fencing. Illawarra advantage: performs well in coastal exposure compared with untreated timber; quick to install on standard levels; long colour life with minimal upkeep.

Chainwire & Security Mesh

Durable mesh fencing for commercial, schools, sports and industrial sites.

Chainwire (chain mesh) fencing is the workhorse of commercial and light industrial boundaries. Galvanised or PVC-coated mesh, with optional barb or razor toppings, delivers high visibility security without blocking airflow or sight lines. We install commercial-height systems for schools, sports fields, factories, compounds and equipment yards across the Illawarra. Posts, rails and tensioning are engineered for long spans and high use. Where appearance matters at street frontage, we can combine chainwire rear/side runs with Colorbond or slat frontages for a professional look. Best for: commercial sites, schools, sports, industrial yards, large rural lots. Illawarra advantage: ideal for Port Kembla industrial sites, school grounds and sports facilities region-wide.

Retaining Wall Systems

Engineered walls for slopes, terraces and usable outdoor space.

Illawarra properties often step from escarpment to coast — which means retaining walls are structural, not decorative. We install concrete sleeper, treated timber, interlocking block and engineered solutions designed for lateral soil loads and coastal rainfall. Proper drainage (ag-pipe, gravel backfill, weep paths) is non-negotiable. Without it, hydrostatic pressure shortens wall life dramatically. Our builds follow good practice for residential retaining and create flat, usable terraces for lawns, decks and entertaining. Best for: sloping blocks, split-level yards, boundary level changes, erosion control. Illawarra advantage: local experience with clay soils, heavy rain events and escarpment slopes.
