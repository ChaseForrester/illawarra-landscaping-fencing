Why Us

Why work with Illawarra Landscaping & Fencing?

  • Local family-style crew — not a faceless franchise
  • Steady pipeline of fencing and landscaping projects
  • Respect for safety, quality and clean sites
  • Opportunity to grow skills across products and services
  • Work across Wollongong, Shellharbour, Kiama and surrounds

What we look for

Reliability, pride in workmanship, a positive attitude and willingness to learn. Trade tickets (White Card, driver’s licence) are highly regarded. Experience in fencing, landscaping, concreting or carpentry is a plus — not always essential.
