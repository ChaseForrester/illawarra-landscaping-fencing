FAQs

Common Questions

Straight answers for Illawarra homeowners comparing fencing and landscaping options.

How much does a new fence cost in the Illawarra?

Price depends on length, height, material (Colorbond, timber, pool, chainwire), access and ground conditions. Most residential boundary jobs are quoted free on-site so you get an accurate figure — not a guess from a calculator. Call 0456 299 538 or use our quote form.

Do you install Colorbond and timber fencing in Wollongong and Shellharbour?

Yes. We supply and install Colorbond®, timber paling, aluminium slat, pool fencing, concrete panel and commercial chainwire across Wollongong, Shellharbour, Kiama and surrounds. See our products page for the full range.

Which suburbs do you cover?

We cover the entire Illawarra region including Wollongong, Shellharbour, Kiama, Dapto, Thirroul, Corrimal, Unanderra, Port Kembla, Fairy Meadow, Figtree, Albion Park and many more. Full list on our coverage page.

Can you do landscaping and fencing in one project?

Absolutely — that’s our strength. Combining fence lines with retaining walls, turf, garden beds and decking means one team, one timeline and a cohesive finish for your outdoor space.

How quickly can you quote and start?

We aim to respond to quote requests within 24 hours. Start dates depend on season and job size; urgent repairs are prioritised where possible. Photo uploads on the form help us quote faster.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are fully licensed and insured for peace of mind on residential and commercial projects across the Illawarra.
